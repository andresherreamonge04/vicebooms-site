Privacy Policy

vicebooms-publisher is an internal tool. It connects only to the vicebooms TikTok and Instagram accounts that we own.

What we collect

  • From TikTok: the basic profile of our own account (open id, display name, avatar) and an access token, used only to upload videos as drafts.
  • From Instagram/Meta: an access token for our own page and Instagram account, used only to publish our clips.

How we use and store it

Tokens are stored privately on our own computer, are never shared or sold, and are used only to upload our clips. We do not collect data from viewers or other users.

Deleting data

We can revoke access at any time from TikTok or Meta settings, and we delete the stored tokens when the tool is no longer used. To ask about this policy, contact us below.
